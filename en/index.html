FIELD BY FIELD

AI in every app

Messages, email, notes or the address bar: the keyboard recognizes the field and adjusts the bottom row and the return key to match. You pick your layout (Turkish Q, English or Spanish QWERTY) in the app. The suggestion strip updates as you type: what you typed in the first slot, completions next to it. You can turn it off in settings; the strip goes away and the keyboard gets shorter.

Plain text field: the return key stays a new-line arrow.

An @ key appears and return becomes Next. Auto-capitalization turns off.

A matching emoji shows up in the suggestion bar for the word you type.

/ and .com keys appear, the emoji and AI keys are hidden, and return becomes Go.

EXCEPTIONS

  • In password fields the AI panel doesn't open and the keyboard records nothing.
  • On iPhone, number and phone fields open the iOS keypad; iOS doesn't allow other keyboards there. On Android, Candemkey shows its own number pad in those fields.

PRIVACY

What we keep and what we don't

What you type isn't stored on our servers.

Not stored on our servers

Text you choose to send is processed by our AI provider. Since October 14, 2026, new request and reply text is not written to Candemsoft debug logs; see the privacy policy for earlier test records.

When does AI run?

Only when you press the AI key and pick an action or send a request. Only on that text: your selection, or if nothing is selected, what's before the cursor.

Kept with your account, if you sign in

Your account details (Apple or Google ID, email, display name), your settings (including custom instructions) and usage counters. The counters hold no text, only the type and time of each action.

Only on your device

Your personal dictionary and background image. You can erase the dictionary with one tap; the image is never sent anywhere.

Password fields

In password and secure fields the keyboard records nothing, and the AI panel doesn't open.

Full Access

iOS

AI features must connect to our server, so iOS requires “Allow Full Access”. It isn't needed for typing; with it off the keyboard keeps working offline. It's optional during setup, and you can skip it.

Android

There's no Full Access setting. When you enable any keyboard, Android shows a standard security warning; Since October 14, 2026, new AI request and reply text is not written to Candemsoft debug logs; see the privacy policy for earlier test records.

In your hands

Export my data copies everything in your account to the clipboard as JSON. Delete account permanently removes your account and all data tied to it.

FAQ

Common questions

AI

What AI model powers Candemkey?

Candemkey utilizes a hybrid AI architecture: local typing, autocomplete, and basic spell check run directly on-device. When you trigger an AI rewrite action, requests are securely handled by state-of-the-art large language models via private encrypted endpoints.

How is Candemkey different from Gboard or SwiftKey?

Traditional keyboards merely suggest the next word based on n-grams. Candemkey operates as an on-keyboard writing assistant that can rewrite entire paragraphs, shift tones to formal or casual, and translate text right in the textfield without app-switching.

What can the AI do?

Press the AI key and the panel shows Ask and Fix plus the Tone menu; Tone has Formal, Casual, Shorten, Expand, Translate and Suggest reply. For anything else, type it in the “Tell Candemkey something…” line, like translating a text or writing a message from scratch in an empty field. Then Apply puts the result in place of your text, Copy sends it to the clipboard, or Retry asks for another version.

Do I have to sign in?

Not to type: the keyboard works without an account. AI features require signing in: with Apple or Google on iPhone, and with Google on Android. AI also needs an internet connection, and Full Access on iPhone.

How does writing style learning work?

This feature is in development for the initial release and is disabled. When enabled in future updates, AI will be able to take brief notes on your writing style that you can edit or delete at any time.

How do safety and content filters work?

Candemkey operates with fixed safety guidelines suitable for all family and work environments; profanity, hate speech, and adult content generation are strictly disabled.

PRIVACY

Is what I type being saved?

The keyboard only sends text when you press the AI key and start an action: your selection, or what's before the cursor. That text goes to our AI provider to produce the reply. Since October 14, 2026, new request and reply text is not written to Candemsoft debug logs; the privacy policy explains earlier test records. If “Learn words” is on, new words you type often are added to the personal dictionary on your device only.

What data do you keep on the server?

If you sign in: your account details, settings, and usage counters (no text). You can export all of it as JSON from the app, or delete your account to remove it permanently. If you don't sign in, there's no account kept for you on the server.

Why does it ask for Full Access?

Only on iPhone, and only for AI. iOS lets a keyboard go online only with “Allow Full Access” on, and AI has to reach our server. It isn't needed for typing: with it off, the keyboard works offline. It's optional during setup, and you can skip it.

Why does Android show a security warning?

Android shows that warning as standard for every keyboard you enable. Since October 14, 2026, new AI request and reply text is not written to Candemsoft debug logs; see the privacy policy for earlier test records.

Does it work in password fields?

In password fields the AI panel doesn't open and the keyboard records nothing. On iPhone, iOS switches to its own keyboard in password fields anyway.

KEYBOARD

Is Candemkey free, and what does Pro include?

Basic text corrections, personal dictionary, and 10 free AI operations daily are completely free. Candemkey Pro will offer expanded daily AI usage (100 requests per day), custom professional/academic styles, and priority server response.

Does it work offline without internet?

Yes. Regular keyboard typing, letter suggestions, personal dictionaries, and layouts for English, Spanish, and Turkish work completely offline. Only cloud AI text rewrites and translations require internet connectivity.

Which languages does it support?

There are three layouts: Turkish Q, English QWERTY and Spanish QWERTY. You choose which ones to use and their order in the app; the first one is used when the keyboard opens. The app and the AI panel are in Turkish, English and Spanish too.

Can I change how the keyboard looks?

Yes. In Keyboard settings you'll find Dark, Light or System theme; Short (default), Normal or Tall height (plus Extra short on Android); your own photo as a background; key borders, one-handed mode and haptic feedback. Key sound is iOS only.

When is it coming out?

There's no firm date yet. Candemkey is being prepared for iOS and Android and isn't in the stores right now. When it is, the App Store and Google Play links will be on this page.
