Privacy and data

Everything Candemkey does with your data: our promises, why Full Access is needed and the full privacy policy.

Our privacy promises

  • What you type stays on your device unless you choose an AI action and send text for processing.
  • Since October 14, 2026, new AI request and reply text is not written to Candemsoft debug logs. Earlier testing records may remain for up to 14 days from collection; see the full policy.
  • In password and secure fields the keyboard records nothing.
  • Statistics never include typed text, words or keystrokes.
  • Your personal dictionary stays on your device and can be erased with one tap.

Anonymous usage statistics

You can turn this on or off on the Privacy and data page in the app; it's off by default. Your text, words or keystrokes are never shared. Nothing is sent for now; the switch stores your choice for statistics that will be added later.

Why is Full Access needed?

AI features must connect to our server, so iOS requires "Allow Full Access". It isn't needed for typing; with it off the keyboard keeps working offline. Text is processed only when you press the AI button. New request and reply text is no longer written to Candemsoft debug logs; the full privacy policy explains earlier test records.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how your data is handled when you use Candemkey (keyboard and app, provided by Candemsoft). By "Candemkey" we mean both the main app you install and the keyboard extension you enable as your system keyboard.

1. Scope

This policy covers the iOS and Android versions of Candemkey. As a guest (not signed in) you can use the core typing features only; creating an account is only needed for the AI features and for syncing your settings across devices.

2. Data tied to your account, stored on our server

  • Account: the unique identifier your sign-in provider gives us when you sign in with Apple or Google, your email address (or the relay address Apple generates if you chose "Hide My Email"), your display name if any, your language preference, and your "I am over 18" declaration.
  • Session info: a refresh token keeps you signed in on your device; we store only a one-way hash of it plus a device label, never the token itself. The token itself lives only in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android encrypted storage).
  • Keyboard and personalization settings: preferences such as layout, appearance, sound/haptics, and AI behavior. If you're signed in, these also sync to our server so they carry over between devices.
  • Memory items: short notes (up to 200 characters, e.g. your name, job, a preference) you add yourself in Personalization > Memory — used as context for your AI requests. If you turn on "Let AI learn my writing style" (off by default, see section 5), short notes the AI derives are added to the same list; both kinds are ordinary items you can see, edit, disable, or delete.
  • AI usage counters: which type of action you performed, how many times, how long it took, and whether it succeeded — never the text of your request or the AI's reply.
  • Subscription/entitlement status: whether you're on the free or Pro plan.
  • Purchase event records: store-reported subscription events and transaction details used for billing support and accounting. Payment card details are handled by Apple or Google.

3. Data that stays on your device unless you choose to send it

  • Everything you type: text, words and keystrokes stay in your device's memory and are not sent over the network unless you deliberately send text by pressing the AI button (section 4). The keyboard never reads or suggests anything in password or secure fields. Temporary debugging logs for AI requests and replies were disabled on October 14, 2026. Text collected in those logs before that date may remain for up to 14 days from collection (section 4).
  • Your personal dictionary: words and corrections learned as you type stay on your device only and never reach our server; you can wipe it completely from Settings with one tap.
  • Keyboard background image: a photo you pick and crop from your gallery stays only on your device (iOS App Group file / Android app directory) and is never sent anywhere. The picker never grants the app access to your whole gallery — it only receives the single image you choose. The only thing stored on the server for this setting is a version number and a dim level, never the image itself.
  • Voice typing (Android): when you tap the keyboard's microphone key, your speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service. If your device supports it, recognition happens entirely on the device; otherwise your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google) may process your voice under its own privacy terms. Your voice is never sent to us or stored by us; the microphone only turns on when you tap the key and turns off when listening ends. The resulting text stays on your device like everything else you type.

4. The AI feature: what's sent, what's stored

AI features (fix, rewrite, free-form commands, etc.) only activate when you tap the AI button on the keyboard. At that moment, the text you want processed (or your free-form instruction), a system instruction describing how the AI should behave, and (if any) your enabled Memory items are sent to our server and from there to our AI provider (OpenAI, based in the US). Candemsoft disabled temporary debugging logs for new AI requests and replies on October 14, 2026. Text collected in those logs before that date may remain for up to 14 days from collection; linked records are deleted if you delete your account. AI usage counters described in section 2 do not contain request or response text. OpenAI's API abuse-monitoring logs may include prompts and responses and are retained for up to 30 days by default.

Reporting an AI response. You can report a response you find inappropriate, harmful or wrong with the flag icon (Report) in the AI panel. A report contains the reason, the type of action and your note, if you wrote one. If you leave "Include the text" on, the AI response and the text it was generated from are sent too; if you turn it off, no text is sent. Reports are used only for review and to improve the service, and are deleted automatically after 12 months; they are deleted immediately if you delete your account. Your reports are included when you export your data.

5. The AI learning your writing style (opt-in, off by default)

If you turn on "Let AI learn my writing style" in Personalization > Memory, roughly every 5th AI request triggers a second, small AI call that holds that single request and response in memory for the duration of that call (never written to disk) and tries to derive a general observation about your writing style (e.g. "prefers short sentences"); most of the time it derives nothing. Only that derived short note (at most 200 characters) gets added to your Memory list. This learning feature does not store the original text; the historical debugging-log exception is described in section 4. You can turn this off at any time; when you do, we will ask whether you would also like to delete what has been learned so far.

6. Why we process your data

To create and manage your account, sync your settings across your devices, provide the AI features, prevent abuse of the service (e.g. exceeding the daily usage limit), fix technical issues, and improve the service.

7. Who we share data with

  • Apple / Google: only during sign-in, to verify your identity. Your tokens are verified only on our server.
  • OpenAI: only when you tap the AI button, as described in sections 4-5.
  • Our hosting provider: all server data physically resides on their infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat: for signed-in users who use subscription features, we send your internal user ID and subscription/purchase information to operate subscriptions. We do not send RevenueCat your email or display name. RevenueCat may also process app/device metadata through its SDK. Payment card details are handled by Apple or Google, not Candemsoft.
  • Currently not connected: ad networks and analytics providers (your consent defaults to off and nothing is sent until you turn it on).

We never sell your data or share it for advertising purposes.

8. International transfers

AI processing happens at OpenAI, located abroad (US). This transfer happens only when you send a request by tapping the AI button, and only for that request.

9. How long we keep data

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (App > Account > Delete account), personal account data, settings, memory items, usage counters, sessions, subscriptions, reports, and associated historical AI debugging logs are deleted immediately and permanently. Purchase event records needed for accounting may remain without their link to your account. Historical AI debugging logs collected before October 14, 2026 are otherwise kept for up to 14 days from collection. AI reports (section 4) are kept for up to 12 months.

10. Security

Only a one-way hash of your session tokens is stored on our side; the token itself stays in your device's secure storage. Communication with our server is encrypted (HTTPS). The keyboard never reads or suggests anything in password or secure text fields.

11. Your rights

You can export your data at any time (App > Account > Export my data — you receive a file containing your profile, settings, memory items, entitlement status, and usage counters) and delete your account. You can request correction of your data or more information via the contact details in section 13. For deletion steps and what is deleted or kept, see the Account deletion page.

12. Children's privacy

Candemkey itself has no specific age requirement; only unlocking flirty/mature content levels requires your "I am over 18" declaration, which is not verified. The app's store age rating will be set separately.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; you'll be notified in-app of material changes.
