Support

Need help with Candemkey (keyboard and app, provided by Candemsoft)? Write to us and we will reply by email.

1. Contact

  • Email: info@candemkey.com
  • Company: Candemsoft
  • Address: YTU Yildiz Technopark Ikitelli Campus, Ikitelli OSB Mah., YTU Ikitelli Teknopark Sok. No:1, 13-A, Basaksehir / Istanbul, Turkey

Please include your device model, iOS or Android version and the Candemkey version (Settings in the app) so we can help faster. Never send your password.

2. Common questions

  • The keyboard does not appear: open Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards (iOS) or the language and input settings (Android), add Candemkey and switch to it with the globe key.
  • AI features do not work: they need an internet connection and a signed-in account, and AI features may require Full Access on iOS. See the privacy page for why.
  • Account or data requests: write to the address above from the email you signed up with, and tell us what you need (access, correction or deletion of your data). For direct self-service steps, see the account deletion page.
  • Reporting a wrong or unsafe AI result: use the report button in the AI panel or email us the text and what went wrong.

3. Response time

We aim to reply within 2 business days.
