SECURITY BY ARCHITECTURE

Privacy-First AI Keyboard

Your keystrokes are never logged or monetized. AI only operates when you explicitly request it, and password fields are strictly protected.

Zero Keystroke Logging

Routine typing remains strictly on your phone. Text is only sent over encrypted connections when you tap the AI key to trigger a rewrite or translation.

Automatic Shielding on Password Fields

AI assistance is automatically locked out when sensitive credentials or credit card inputs are active.

No Ad Tracking or Data Brokering

Candemkey does not build advertising profiles or sell data to third parties. We are funded by transparent software subscriptions.

Complete Data Ownership & Deletion

Export your settings anytime in JSON, or permanently wipe your account and all associated tokens with a single tap.
