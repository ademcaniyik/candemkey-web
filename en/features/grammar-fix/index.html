AI PROOFREADING ON THE GO

Smart Grammar & Spell Check Keyboard

Eliminate typos, punctuation mistakes, and awkward phrasing instantly with deep contextual AI editing right on your keyboard.

One-Tap Proofreading in Any App

No need to copy text and jump into ChatGPT or web editors. While typing in Slack, WhatsApp, or Gmail, simply tap the AI key and hit Fix. Your text is refined and corrected in place.

Deep Contextual Understanding

Traditional autocorrect replaces words blindly based on key distance. Candemkey understands the entire sentence nuance, catching tricky homophones, missing commas, and tense mismatches.

Preview and One-Click Apply

Review the exact edits proposed by the model. Tap Apply to substitute the clean version into your textfield, or tap Retry for another wording variation.

Multilingual Support

Write with confidence in English, Spanish, and Turkish. Candemkey seamlessly switches between grammar engines based on your active keyboard layout.
